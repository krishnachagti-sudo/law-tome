# Release schedule: 2 new books a week

Set by Krishna on 2026-11-12: two new titles every week from the week of
Monday 16 November 2026. KDP allows up to 2 new titles per format per week
(help page G202172740, checked 2026-11-11), so each week is two ebooks plus
two paperbacks; hardcovers count separately.

## The weekly rhythm

Every book moves through the same five stages. Books are worked on in parallel,
so each week two books ship while the next four are in progress.

| When | Stage | Done means |
|---|---|---|
| T−3 weeks | Brief and outline | Krishna-approved brief; frozen outline |
| T−2 weeks | Draft and art | All entries drafted; all images generated; manuscript checker passes |
| T−1 week | Checks | Every verdict, quote and date checked against a primary source; legal read on living people; second reviewer on the art contact sheet; proof ordered |
| T−2 days | Approval | Krishna reads the PDF and cover, says yes. Nothing is uploaded without it: the book goes out in his name |
| T (Fri) | Publish | Upload ebook + paperback; AI disclosure ticked; listing, A+ and series set; errata page live |

Week 1 is compressed because nothing was in the pipe; from week 3 the rhythm
above holds. Proof copies take 1–2 weeks to reach India, so early books are
uploaded after the online previewer and fixed by file update when the proof
arrives.

## Ship gate (every book, every week)

A book that misses any of these slips a week rather than shipping:

1. Manuscript checker passes (`books/scripts/check-manuscript.mjs --strict`).
2. Every factual claim traced to a source in the record; every verdict and quote checked against a primary or the best available source, logged in the book's QA log.
3. Living people: legal wording pass done.
4. Art: every image passes the 12-point QA list; second reviewer has seen the contact sheet.
5. Cover, description, keywords and categories written; AI disclosure ready.
6. Every web address printed in the book is live: for True or Folklore?, conyso.com/lawtome/errata, /bonus (with the bonus round written and the email signup working) and /books.
7. Krishna has approved the final PDF and cover.
