# ISBN application: True or Folklore? (paperback)

Krishna chose his own ISBN with the Conyso Publishing imprint (16 Nov 2026).
ISBNs in India are free from the Raja Rammohun Roy National Agency for ISBN,
applied for on https://isbn.gov.in. Registration needs your own account, a
government photo ID (PAN, Aadhaar, voter ID or passport) and address proof, so
you do this step yourself. Claude can't enter ID numbers or passwords.

Approval times from third-party sources range from 3–5 days to 2–4 weeks;
the portal doesn't state one. Apply early: KDP launch is planned for the week of
16 Nov 2026.

## One ISBN per format

The paperback and the Kindle ebook each need their own ISBN. KDP doesn't require
an ISBN for the ebook (it assigns an ASIN), so apply for the paperback now.
A hardcover later would need a third.

## After it's issued

Tell Claude the number. It goes on the copyright page and the barcode on the back
cover, and gets entered in KDP under "Use my own ISBN" with imprint Conyso Publishing.
