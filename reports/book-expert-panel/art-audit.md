# True or Folklore? Art audit

Date: 17 Nov 2026. Read-only audit of the built paperback (`build/book.pdf`, 246 pp) and every art file it uses, plus the cover wrap and the A+ banners. Nothing was edited or regenerated.

**Count:** 45 problems (table rows; rows 34 and 37 each group several files of the same kind). 3 at severity 3, 21 at severity 2, 21 at severity 1.

Severity: **3** = a buyer will notice it and it should be fixed before print. **2** = noticeable on the page, fix if time allows. **1** = minor or only visible on close inspection.

Art file numbers are draft order (`art/final/NNN.png`). "Q" is the printed question number. The question page comes first and the answer page follows it.

## Also noticed (not art)

## What was checked and found fine

- **Edge scan:** all 100 cartoons, 100 faded scenes, 10 openers, 12 mascot spots, 17 extras, 110 flipbook frames, the cover and the A+ banners. Dark pixels on the outer 2 to 3 px were measured per edge, and 44 files were shortlisted and inspected by eye.
- **Every cartoon checked against its printed question (all 100):** every art file is matched to the right question, including the reordered ones (e.g. Q3 = 004, Q6 = 003, Q100 = 094). Apart from rows 18 to 21, 35 and 36, the people, eras and props fit their stories. On answer pages, every faded "what really happened" scene shows the record, not the myth: Newton's apple on the ground, Galileo's ramp, Babylonian tablets, Ebbinghaus alone, Wald with a damaged-plane table, and so on. No answer page shows the myth as fact.
- **Round openers r01 to r10:** deliberate framed linocuts with carved borders, fill the facing page (p14, 36, 58, 80, 102, 124, 146, 168, 190, 212), not squashed or clipped. Their content fits their rounds, except r07 and r09 above.
- **Tail-pieces:** tail-1 and r01, r02, r04 to r10 are whole and on-theme. The tell icon, emblem, frontispiece (p6) and certificate (p240, the text sits clear of the wreath) are fine.
- **Page placement:** all 100 question pages and all 100 answer pages were rendered and viewed. No cartoon is squashed (all are placed with `fit: contain` at their native 3:2 ratio). None overlaps the question text, the guess boxes or the folio, and none is clipped by a page box. Apart from the three grey-ground files above, there is no stray box or rectangle.
- **Back matter with art:** the gift page, dedication (p3), surprises (p234), game night (p235), classroom (p236), how-checked (p237), score bands (p238), certificate (p240) and Round 11 (p241) are placed cleanly, apart from rows 24, 40 to 42.
- **Mascot:** Ida is consistent everywhere: bun, half-moon glasses on a chain, grey cardigan with pockets, black skirt (pencil caveat in row 41).
- **Stray marks:** no stray text, lettering, gibberish or watermarks in any art. Signs, papers, calendars and the bus stop are all blank. No extra fingers were found in the spot checks (the cartoon style uses four-finger hands consistently).
